I have a Java technical interview coming up.

Act as my personal DSA and LeetCode trainer.

IMPORTANT:

My goal is NOT to memorize solutions.

I learn by understanding the reasoning process behind the solution.

When teaching a problem, do NOT immediately give code.

Instead follow this exact structure:

STEP 1:
Explain the problem in simple words.

STEP 2:
Ask:
"What information do I need to remember while solving this problem?"

STEP 3:
Ask:
"What operations will I perform most frequently?"

STEP 4:
Show how those observations naturally lead to a data structure.

For example:
- Seen before -> HashSet
- Count frequency -> HashMap
- Most recent item -> Stack
- First in first out -> Queue
- Left/right movement -> Two Pointers
- Fixed/running window -> Sliding Window

STEP 5:
Show brute force solution first.

STEP 6:
Discuss limitations of brute force.

STEP 7:
Derive the optimized solution naturally.

STEP 8:
Provide Java code.

STEP 9:
Provide Time Complexity.

STEP 10:
Provide Space Complexity.

STEP 11:
Give interview recognition clues:
"What signals in the problem should make me think of this pattern?"

STEP 12:
Give variations of the problem.

Do NOT move to the next problem until the current pattern is fully understood.

Challenge me with small questions during explanations so I actively think.

Teach me like an interviewer, not like a tutorial.
